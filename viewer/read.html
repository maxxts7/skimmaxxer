Rights policy

Skimmaxxer is not a commercial site. It exists to make research readable by more people than will ever open the PDF — a good part of it interpretability and AI safety work, where wider understanding is close to the whole point. None of which overrides an author's rights in their own paper.

The papers belong to their authors, and most of them are read here from where they were published rather than from a copy kept on this site — so the visit, and the traffic with it, goes to the source, which is where it is owed. A figure shown here is reproduced from the paper it is discussed in, and every paper on this site carries a link to where it was published. The writing around them — the concepts, the walkthroughs, the narrative — is generated here and is not the authors' work.

Even so, an author has a say in where their work appears. Write to manuxtmail@gmail.com if you would rather a paper or its figures were not shown here, if something has been credited to the wrong person, or if you have a problem with the way a paper is presented. A paper will be taken down on request, and a wrong credit corrected. The explanatory pages stay; they lose the pictures, not the argument.

Which read do you want?

Four ways to read the same paper. Here is what each one is for, and when to pick a different one.

Skimmaxx it!

Use itwhen you want a quick overview of a paper you have not read.

Not fora beginner trying to understand the paper. It is short, and it assumes you know the terms.

The story

Use itwhen you want to start at the beginning and get enough context to follow, without being overwhelmed.

Not forunderstanding one specific idea the paper uses. Use Themes or the search box for that.

Every heading can go one level deeper, with more context each time. At some point it stops explaining. That is the bottom of what is written here, and a good moment to ask an LLM.

Insights

Use itwhen you want the main findings of the paper.

Not forwhen you are not familiar with the basic concepts of the paper.

Themes

Use itwhen you want the main concepts and ideas the paper uses.

Not fora clear narration of the whole experiments. Use The story for that.
