Rights policy

The papers belong to their authors. A figure shown here is reproduced from the paper it is discussed in, and every paper on this site carries a link to where it was published. The writing around them — the concepts, the walkthroughs, the narrative — is generated here and is not the authors' work.

If you hold the rights to a paper and would rather its figures, or the paper itself, were not shown here, email manuxtmail@gmail.com and they will be taken down. The explanatory pages stay; they lose the pictures, not the argument.
