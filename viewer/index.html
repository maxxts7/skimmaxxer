Why not the PDF, or an LLM

AI safety papers are usually prerequisite-heavy, so it is easy to come across unfamiliar concepts and ideas while reading one. Sometimes the paper gives you some context for what is going on, but usually that is not thorough enough. This makes it difficult for a researcher who is not familiar with that particular prerequisite concept to carry on reading. This is especially true for beginners trying to come into AI safety. Skimmaxxer exists to make skimming a technical paper as frictionless as possible.

  • A paper is written for experts active in the field.
  • It explains a new contribution and its context. For the rest of the prerequisites, you need to read the cited papers.
  • Reading is constantly blocked by concepts you haven't seen before.
  • Reading past unknown concepts gets costlier as you progress, because the next section builds on what you skipped.
  • So it's hard to make progress even when you put in the time, because your effort is diluted by researching prerequisites.
  • You often don't have the background knowledge to ask the right questions.
  • Every question you have about the paper costs you the time it takes the LLM to reply.
  • You usually try to understand one concept at a time and sometimes lose the big picture.
  • When the paper is prerequisite-heavy, you end up with multiple chats open at once and waste time switching between them.
  • It's simply not an interface optimized for reading a prerequisite-heavy technical paper.
  • The paper is presented several ways at once: as a summary, as a detailed explanation, with its figures broken down, and as individual concepts. So you can always come at it from whichever side you like.
  • Every term the paper takes for granted already has its own write-up.
  • Each concept gets a page. Every term on that page gets one too, and so on, as deep as you need to go.
  • Most of the work of aggregating information is already done for you.

The point is not that this is faster. It is that the time you spend here adds up. You are never stopped by something you do not know, and you are never waiting for an answer. By the end of an hour, you understand things you did not understand at the start.

LLM policy

Every page here is generated from research papers by a large language model — the concepts, the walkthroughs of each figure, the narrative and its levels. None of it is written by hand, and none of it is peer-reviewed.

What that buys you is traceability rather than authority: every claim carries a link to the place in the paper it was drawn from, so anything here can be checked against the paper itself. Read it as a way into a paper, not as a substitute for reading one.
