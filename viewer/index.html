Why not the PDF, or an LLM

AI safety papers are usually prerequisite-heavy, so it is easy to come across unfamiliar concepts and ideas while reading one. Sometimes the paper gives you some context for what is going on, but usually that is not thorough enough. This makes it difficult for a researcher who is not familiar with that particular prerequisite concept to carry on reading. This is especially true for beginners trying to come into AI safety. Skimmaxxer exists to make skimming a technical paper as frictionless as possible.

  • A paper is written for people who already work in the field.
  • It explains a few things. For the rest, it points you at another paper.
  • So you reach a word you do not know, and you stop.
  • You cannot read past it, because the next section is built on that word.
  • This is why an hour with a paper can end where it started.
  • You do not know enough of the paper to ask the right questions.
  • Every prompt is another wait.
  • You usually try to understand one concept at a time, and sometimes end up missing the big picture.
  • When the paper is prerequisite-heavy, you end up having multiple chats open at the same time and find yourself wasting time switching back and forth between them.
  • It is simply the wrong interface for reading a long technical paper.
  • Every term the paper uses has been written out already.
  • Each one is its own page. The terms inside that page are pages too, as far down as you need.
  • So there is nothing to ask for, and nothing to wait for.
  • Each read says at the start what it takes for granted. That is the only thing you have to bring.
  • And the paper is laid out several ways at once, so you can always come at it from another side.

The point is not that this is faster. It is that the time you spend here adds up. You are never stopped by something you do not know, and you are never waiting for an answer. By the end of an hour, you understand things you did not understand at the start.

Where this is going

Right now I add the papers by hand. Most of what is below is about getting myself out of that loop, and then about what the shelf can do once adding a paper costs almost nothing. There are no dates here — what I am promising is the order, not the calendar.

  1. Now

    Manual runs

    When you ask for a paper it lands in a queue only I can see. I start the run myself, and the machine takes a few hours over it. The way you find out it is done is that the paper turns up on the shelf.

  2. Next

    Automatic runs

    Asking for a paper sets the work going there and then, with nobody in the middle. Those 24 hours stop being how long it takes me to notice, and become simply how long the run takes.

  3. After that

    Visible progress

    A paper being read stops going quiet on you. The page says where it has got to — figures cropped, concepts pulled out, the story written — so you can tell a request that is still working from one that fell over.

  4. After that

    An open queue

    You would be able to see what has been asked for, what is being read right now, and what is still waiting. And if you ask for a paper somebody else has already asked for, that adds weight to theirs instead of starting a second run.

  5. Later

    Adjustable levels

    How much a read assumes of you is one setting today, and I am the one who fixes it, once per paper. Handing that dial to you would let the same paper work for somebody meeting the field and for somebody who already works in it, without either of you reading it twice.

  6. Later

    A connected shelf

    Every paper already explains what it needs on its own pages, and points across when another paper here covers the same ground. If you could follow those links both ways, the shelf would start to feel like one connected body of work rather than a row of separate reads.

LLM policy

Every page here is generated from research papers by a large language model — the concepts, the walkthroughs of each figure, the narrative and its levels. None of it is written by hand, and none of it is peer-reviewed.

What that buys you is traceability rather than authority: every claim carries a link to the place in the paper it was drawn from, so anything here can be checked against the paper itself. Read it as a way into a paper, not as a substitute for reading one.
