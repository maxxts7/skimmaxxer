NAME: Training the thing

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Everything needed to reproduce a trained model: the translation datasets, subword vocabularies built with byte-pair encoding or word pieces, batching by sequence length, and eight GPUs on one machine. The learning rate is not fixed — it ramps up linearly for the first 4000 steps and then decays as the inverse square root of the step. Label smoothing, dropout, and averaging the last several checkpoints round out the recipe, and the code was released as tensor2tensor.

  - label-smoothing | Label smoothing [major]: A training target modification: instead of putting probability 1 on the correct token, the target gives it 1 - eps and spreads eps = 0.1 over other tokens, penalizing overconfidence.
  - checkpoint-averaging | Checkpoint averaging [minor]: The final model's weights are the elementwise average of the last several saved checkpoints: 5 for base models, 20 for big models, written at 10-minute intervals.
