THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Constituency parsing is the second task, used to check whether the architecture generalizes past translation. It only works as a sequence transduction problem because parse trees are flattened into token sequences by the cited linearization scheme. Table 4 reports WSJ Section 23 F1 in two regimes: trained on the roughly 40K-sentence WSJ treebank alone, and trained semi-supervised on about 17M additional machine-parsed sentences from the cited corpora. The paper reports beating the BerkeleyParser and most prior work with little task-specific tuning, and names RNNG as the one model it does not beat.
