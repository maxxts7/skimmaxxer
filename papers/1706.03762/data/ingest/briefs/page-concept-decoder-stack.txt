SUMMARY (already shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat it verbatim):
The Transformer's decoder: $N=6$ identical layers, each with masked self-attention, attention over the encoder output, and a feed-forward sub-layer.

DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The decoder is also a stack of $N = 6$ identical layers. Each layer has the same two sub-layers as an encoder layer, plus a third sub-layer inserted between them that performs multi-head attention over the encoder stack's output (encoder-decoder attention). As in the encoder, every sub-layer is wrapped in a residual connection followed by layer normalization. The decoder's self-attention sub-layer is modified with a mask so positions cannot attend to later positions; combined with shifting the output embeddings right by one position, this guarantees the prediction for position $i$ depends only on known outputs at positions before $i$.
