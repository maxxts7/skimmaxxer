NAME: Why self-attention, compared to the alternatives

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The paper's argument for the swap, made on three criteria: computation per layer, how many operations must run in sequence rather than in parallel, and the longest path a signal travels between two positions. On the third, self-attention connects any two positions in a constant number of steps, which the paper says should make long-range dependencies easier to learn. For very long sequences it sketches a restricted variant where each position attends only to a nearby window. This sits here because it needs both self-attention and the recurrent and convolutional baselines already on the table.
