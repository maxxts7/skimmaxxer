THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Table 2 carries the headline result on WMT 2014 English-German and English-French: the big Transformer's BLEU against prior models including ConvS2S and ensembles, plotted against an estimated training cost, so quality and compute are the two axes. Table 3 varies one thing at a time on the base model — head count in rows (A), key size d_k in rows (B), size and dropout in (C) and (D), positional encoding in (E). The paper reads these as: one head is worse than eight but too many heads also drop off, shrinking d_k hurts and may mean the dot product is too simple a compatibility function, removing residual dropout costs quality, and learned positions make no real difference. Label smoothing is the case where the metrics disagree — it makes perplexity worse and BLEU better, and the paper keeps it. Figures 4 and 5 are offered as suggestive, not conclusive: two heads splitting the reference of "its", and two heads in one layer with visibly different patterns.
