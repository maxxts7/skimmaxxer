NAME: Putting order back in

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Self-attention treats a sequence as a set, so the model has no idea what order its tokens are in. The paper's fix is a positional encoding added to the input embeddings, and the version it uses is the sinusoid bank of Equation (4) — sines and cosines whose wavelengths form a geometric progression. It also tries learned positional embeddings as the alternative. The paper reports nearly identical results for the two and keeps the sinusoids, hedging that they may extrapolate to sequences longer than those seen in training. This is the one place where the design has to add back something recurrence supplied for free.
