NAME: From one dot product to eight heads

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
These edges build the attention mechanism from the inside out. Equation (1) defines scaled dot-product attention, which is plain dot-product attention plus one divisor, 1/sqrt(d_k); the paper says it picks the dot product over additive attention for speed, and adds the divisor because large d_k pushes the softmax into a flat region. The MultiHead equation then calls Equation (1) h times in parallel, each head working at width d_k = d_v = d_model/h, which is why h = 8 and 64-wide heads go together. The claim for running h heads instead of one is that a single attention output is an average, and averaging blurs what individual subspaces could have kept separate. Figure 2 draws both halves: Equation (1) as a data-flow box on the left, the h parallel heads on the right.
