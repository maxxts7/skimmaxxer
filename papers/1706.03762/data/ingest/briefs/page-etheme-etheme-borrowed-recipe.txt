NAME: The recipe is mostly borrowed

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Much of the training setup is taken from other papers and used at stated settings rather than re-derived. Weight tying comes from the output-embedding work — one matrix in three roles, encoder input, decoder input, and pre-softmax projection — with the sqrt(d_model) multiplier applied on top. Label smoothing is the cited LSR target mixture at eps = 0.1; the subword vocabularies are BPE with its merge loop and joint source-target vocabulary for English-German, and a 32000-token word-piece vocabulary for English-French; the beam-search length penalty is the cited normalizer set to alpha = 0.6. What the paper adds of its own is the schedule: Equation (3) ramps the learning rate linearly for warmup_steps = 4000 and then decays it as the inverse square root, scaled by d_model, which overrides Adam's own step size. The reported training cost is not measured directly but estimated from GPU count, wall-clock time, and an assumed throughput.
