NAME: The Problem and the Open Questions

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The paper starts from an imbalance: raw text is plentiful, while labelled data for any particular language understanding task is small and costly to produce. It frames the diverse supervised tasks it cares about under one label, and asks whether one model trained on unlabelled text could serve them all. Two questions are left open at this point and the rest of the paper answers them: which unsupervised objective produces representations worth transferring, and how those representations should be moved into a target task. This sits first because every later choice is a response to one of these two questions.
