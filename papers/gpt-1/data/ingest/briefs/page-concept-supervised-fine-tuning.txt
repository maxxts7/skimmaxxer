NAME: Supervised Fine-Tuning (Stage Two)

SUMMARY (already shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat it verbatim):
Run labelled inputs through the pre-trained model, take the final block's activation at the last token, and train a linear layer on it to predict the label.

DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The labelled dataset $C$ holds instances made of a token sequence $x_1,\dots,x_m$ and a label $y$. The whole sequence is passed through the pre-trained transformer.

The activation of the final transformer block at the last token, written $h_l^m$, is taken as the representation of the instance.

A linear layer with parameters $W_y$ maps that vector to one score per class, and a softmax turns the scores into $P(y \mid x_1,\dots,x_m)$.

Training maximizes $L_2(C)$, the summed log-probability of the correct labels, with gradients flowing back through the entire pre-trained stack. The pre-trained representations are adjusted by the task, not merely consulted by it.

The word discriminative marks the change of objective: the model now predicts a label given an input rather than predicting the input itself.

This stage is short. The paper reports a learning rate of 6.25e-5, batches of 32, and three epochs sufficient for most tasks.

THEME IT BELONGS TO: theme-stage-two-fine-tuning-and-input-shapes | Stage Two: Fine-Tuning and Reshaping Task Inputs - Stage two runs a labelled input through the pre-trained stack, takes the final block's activation at the last token, and multiplies it by one added matrix to score labels. The language modelling loss is not dropped: it keeps being optimized alongside the task loss, scaled by a weight the paper sets to 0.5. Tasks whose inputs have several parts are handled by writing those parts into one ordered sequence with a start token, an end token and a delimiter between parts, rather than by building an architecture that mirrors the structure. The chapter ends with the settings actually used, which are the pre-training settings with a smaller learning rate and three passes over the task data.
