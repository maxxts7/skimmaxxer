NAME: Corpus, Tokens and Training Setup

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The corpus is over 7,000 unpublished books, chosen because the text stays in its original order across sentences and paragraphs; the paper contrasts it with a same-sized benchmark that is shuffled sentence by sentence. Raw text is cleaned, tokenized, then cut into subword units by byte-pair encoding with 40,000 merges. Training uses Adam with a learning rate warmed up over 2000 updates and then annealed on a cosine curve, for 100 passes over the corpus, with dropout and a decoupled weight decay. The reported perplexity of 18.4 is offered as evidence the model fit the corpus, not as a result about any task; it sits here rather than in the results chapter for that reason.
