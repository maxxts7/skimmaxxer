DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The paper's stated default is inheritance: unless it says otherwise, the fine-tuning run keeps the pre-training hyperparameters.

The exceptions are small. A fresh dropout at rate 0.1 is added to the classifier head. Most tasks use a learning rate of 6.25e-5, roughly a quarter of the pre-training maximum, with a batch size of 32.

Training is short. The paper reports that the model finetunes quickly and that 3 epochs sufficed for most cases.

The schedule is linear decay with warmup over 0.2% of training, and the auxiliary language modelling objective is weighted at $\lambda = 0.5$.

The short, low-rate schedule is part of the paper's claim that adapting a pre-trained model to a task is cheap compared with training one. Note the hedge: most cases, not all.
