DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
BooksCorpus is the unlabelled text the language model is trained on. It contains over 7,000 unique unpublished books spanning genres such as Adventure, Fantasy and Romance.

The property the paper cares about is not the genre mix but the shape of the text. A book arrives as a long unbroken run of sentences in their original order.

That matters because the objective asks the model to predict the next token from the tokens before it. If the preceding tokens reach back across paragraphs, the model has an incentive to condition on long-range information rather than on the last few words.

The paper calls this out as its reason for preferring BooksCorpus to a similarly sized but sentence-shuffled alternative.

It reports a token-level perplexity of 18.4 on this corpus as a sanity figure for the pre-training run, and does not tie that number to any downstream claim.

THEME IT BELONGS TO: theme-corpus-tokens-and-training-setup | Corpus, Tokens and Training Setup - The corpus is over 7,000 unpublished books, chosen because the text stays in its original order across sentences and paragraphs; the paper contrasts it with a same-sized benchmark that is shuffled sentence by sentence. Raw text is cleaned, tokenized, then cut into subword units by byte-pair encoding with 40,000 merges. Training uses Adam with a learning rate warmed up over 2000 updates and then annealed on a cosine curve, for 100 passes over the corpus, with dropout and a decoupled weight decay. The reported perplexity of 18.4 is offered as evidence the model fit the corpus, not as a result about any task; it sits here rather than in the results chapter for that reason.
