NAME: The Scoreboard and Its Gaps

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The evaluation is twelve datasets across four families — natural language inference, question answering and commonsense reasoning, semantic similarity, and text classification — and the headline is new best published results on nine of them, reported as absolute percentage-point differences against the previous best. On inference the fine-tuned model leads task-built baselines on four of five datasets; on the reading-and-reasoning benchmarks it gains 8.9 on Story Cloze and 5.7 on RACE over every previously published system; on the remaining GLUE tasks it takes CoLA, STS-B and QQP, with an overall GLUE score of 72.8 against 68.9. CoLA is the largest single jump, 45.4 against a previous best of 35.0, and SST-2 sits beside it as the contrast — 91.3 percent is competitive but does not beat the state of the art. The gaps are where the general claim thins: RTE is the one inference dataset it loses and one of the three it misses overall, and RTE has 2490 training examples against SNLI's roughly 550k, which invites the small-data explanation — except that STS-B, smaller still at about 5.7k, is one of the wins.
