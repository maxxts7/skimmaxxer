DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
Semi-supervised learning uses both labelled and unlabelled data for the same end task. The related-work section says this is the category the paper broadly falls under.

It then sorts prior work into a rough chronology. The earliest methods computed word- and phrase-level statistics from unlabelled text and fed them into a supervised model as extra features.

Later work replaced those hand-built counts with learned word embeddings, and more recent work moved beyond single words to phrase and sentence representations.

The paper's own contribution sits at the far end of that progression. It transfers an entire pre-trained network rather than a table of vectors or a fixed feature channel.
