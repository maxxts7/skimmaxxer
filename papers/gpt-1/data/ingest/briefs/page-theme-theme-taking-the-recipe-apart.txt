NAME: Taking the Recipe Apart

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Three diagnostics ask which parts of the recipe are doing the work. Transferring more pre-trained layers keeps helping rather than saturating early, and hand-built heuristics show the pre-trained model already doing several tasks at some level with no supervised training at all, improving steadily as pre-training runs. The removals are blunter: no pre-training costs the most, swapping the transformer for an LSTM costs less, and dropping the auxiliary language modelling loss helps on some datasets and hurts on others. The paper's reading of why attention transfers better than recurrence is stated as a hypothesis, and the zero-shot heuristics are hand-designed, so both carry less weight than the ablation numbers.
