=== MAJOR CONCEPTS (each has a page; link the first mention) ===
  labeled-data-scarcity | Scarcity of Labelled Data: The problem the paper starts from: raw text is abundant, but human-annotated data for any one task is small and expensive.
  semi-supervised-learning-for-nlp | Semi-Supervised Learning for NLP: The family of methods that combine a large unlabelled corpus with a small labelled dataset, which the paper places itself inside.
  two-stage-training-procedure | Two-Stage Training Procedure: The paper's recipe: learn the network's initial parameters with a language modelling objective on unlabelled text, then adapt those same parameters to each target task wi
  generative-pretraining | Generative Pre-Training (Stage One): Train a multi-layer transformer decoder to predict each next token on a large unlabelled corpus, and keep the weights rather than the outputs.
  language-modeling-objective | Language Modelling Objective $L_1$: The pre-training loss $L_1(U)=\sum_i \log P(u_i \mid u_{i-k},\dots,u_{i-1};\Theta)$: maximize the log-probability the model gives each token given the tokens before it.
  multi-layer-transformer-decoder | Multi-Layer Transformer Decoder: The language model is a stack of transformer blocks used as a decoder only: masked multi-head self-attention over the context, then position-wise feedforward layers.
  decoder-only-stack | Decoder-Only Stack: The model drops the encoder half of the original encoder-decoder transformer and keeps one stack that attends only to its own past tokens.
  masked-self-attention | Masked Self-Attention: Self-attention restricted so each position attends only to itself and earlier positions, never to later ones.
  supervised-fine-tuning | Supervised Fine-Tuning (Stage Two): Run labelled inputs through the pre-trained model, take the final block's activation at the last token, and train a linear layer on it to predict the label.
  auxiliary-lm-objective | Auxiliary Language Modelling Objective: During fine-tuning the paper keeps optimizing the language modelling loss alongside the task loss: $L_3(C) = L_2(C) + \lambda\, L_1(C)$.
  task-specific-input-transformations | Task-Specific Input Transformations: Structured task inputs are rewritten as one ordered token sequence, so the pre-trained model can read them without architectural change.
  task-agnostic-model | Task-Agnostic Model: A single architecture, pre-trained once, applied to every task without being redesigned for any of them.
  bookscorpus | BooksCorpus: The pre-training corpus: over 7,000 unpublished books across genres. Its selling point in this paper is long stretches of contiguous text.
  model-specification | Model Specification: The exact architecture trained: a 12-layer decoder-only transformer with 768-dimensional states, 12 attention heads and 3072-dimensional inner feed-forward states.
  pre-training-optimization | Pre-Training Optimization Setup: How the language model was actually trained: Adam, a warmup-then-cosine schedule peaking at 2.5e-4, and 100 epochs of 64-sequence minibatches.
  fine-tuning-hyperparameters | Fine-Tuning Hyperparameters: Fine-tuning reuses the pre-training settings except for a learning rate of 6.25e-5, batch size 32, three epochs, classifier dropout 0.1, and a short warmup.
  evaluation-task-families | Four Task Families, Twelve Datasets: The paper evaluates on natural language inference, question answering and commonsense reasoning, semantic similarity, and text classification, across twelve datasets.
  layers-transferred | Impact of Number of Layers Transferred: An analysis varying how many pre-trained layers are carried into fine-tuning, showing each transformer layer adds performance, up to 9% on MultiNLI for full transfer.
  zero-shot-behaviors | Zero-Shot Behaviours: Heuristics that make the pre-trained generative model perform the evaluation tasks with no supervised fine-tuning at all, tracked across pre-training.
  ablation-studies | Ablation Studies: Three controlled removals reported in Table 5: dropping the auxiliary LM objective, replacing the transformer with an LSTM, and skipping pre-training entirely.
