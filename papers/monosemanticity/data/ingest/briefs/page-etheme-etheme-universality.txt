PAGE FOR EDGE-THEME: etheme-universality
NAME: The same features in a second model

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
To ask whether a feature is a fact about the model rather than about one training run, the paper trains a second one-layer transformer with the same architecture, data and hyperparameters, differing only in random seed, and runs dictionary learning on it separately. Features are matched by taking the highest Pearson correlation of activations over 40 million dataset examples, and the Arabic feature comes back in the second dictionary. Across the whole population the median match is 0.72 for features against 0.46 for neurons, which is the paper's main quantitative claim here. Agreement on when matched features fire is higher than agreement on what they do, since logit weight similarity is lower — two features can be firing on the same tokens while writing differently to the output. Weighting a feature's logit effects by whether it was actually active and by the token that actually came next brings much of that agreement back.
