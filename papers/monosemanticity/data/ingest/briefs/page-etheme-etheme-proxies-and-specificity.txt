PAGE FOR EDGE-THEME: etheme-proxies-and-specificity
NAME: An outside yardstick for what a feature responds to

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
Specificity is the claim that a feature firing implies its hypothesized context is present, and the paper treats it as the more important half of the case, above sensitivity. Establishing it needs a score computed without reference to the feature: a proxy of the form log of P(string given the hypothesis) over P(string), asking how much more likely that text is under a guessed context than under text in general. For a writing system the hard term is inverted with Bayes' rule, so that scoring 'is this Arabic' reduces to checking which Unicode block each character falls in; for character sets like base64 and DNA the conditional is modeled as a uniform draw over the allowed characters. Plotting a feature's activation histogram with every column split by the proxy gives a spectrum rather than one number — the Arabic feature's strongest activations are close to purely Arabic and its weak ones much less so — and the paper argues the strong end is what matters, because a feature's effect on the logits is roughly linear in its activation. Where feature and proxy disagree the paper is often willing to blame the proxy, and says so plainly: the base64 feature correlates only 0.38 with its own yardstick, and hexadecimal strings are counted as base64 by a proxy that is simply too broad.
