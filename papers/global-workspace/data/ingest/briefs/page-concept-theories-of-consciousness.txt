DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The paper frames its results in global workspace terms because that is the theory its experiments were designed around, but the discussion relates the findings to other functional theories as well, checking each theory's characteristic signature against the J-space. The scope is explicitly limited to theories that tie consciousness to functional or computational properties; theories that hinge on physical causal structure or biological substrate are set aside as beyond what computational experiments can address. The paper suggests its results may influence the theories themselves, by exhibiting a system with some properties of conscious access realized through mechanisms differing from those the theories posit, and closes by proposing language models as an experimentally tractable system for studying questions about consciousness that are hard even to pose in brains.
