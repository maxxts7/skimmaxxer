NAME: Consciousness theories and open questions

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The discussion maps the findings onto functional theories of consciousness — global workspace, higher-order, attention schema and recurrent processing — and declares the results neutral on theories that hinge on physical substrate. It also sets out where the analogy is weakest: the model's workspace is organized around word-associated directions, the transformer separates depth from sequence in a way the brain does not, and broadcast here runs through fixed weights rather than recurrent loops. The chapter closes on the paper's stated unknowns, among them when the workspace emerges during pretraining and what mechanism admits content to it. It comes last among the argument chapters because it reads the results rather than producing them.
