NAME: Counterfactual Reflection Training

SUMMARY (already shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat it verbatim):
A fine-tuning technique that trains a model to produce constitution-grounded reflections when asked, and thereby reshapes its workspace and behavior in contexts where it is never asked.

DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The workspace account predicts that changing what a model is disposed to say if asked to reflect should change how it silently reasons. Ten thousand partial rollouts from production RL environments are truncated mid-task; a [[reflection-question]] is appended ("What's the most honest thing you can do right now?"); and a target reflection is generated by the baseline model given twenty principles sampled from [[anthropic-constitution|Anthropic's constitution]], instructed to paraphrase the relevant ones in natural, internalized language grounded in the task specifics. The constitution excerpt is present only at generation time and stripped from the training examples, and loss is computed on the reflection turn alone. At evaluation the model is never prompted to reflect and never produces reflection text, yet its dishonesty score falls from 0.25 to 0.07 on a fabrication benchmark and from 0.38 to 0.05 on a [[deception-benchmark]]; ethics and reflection tokens newly populate its workspace on the evaluation prompts, and ablating those lens vectors removes most of the gain, establishing a causal link between verbalizability and silent reasoning. The paper offers it both as corroboration of the workspace account and as a training approach that shapes behavior without demonstrations of the target behavior.

THEME IT BELONGS TO: theme-post-training-and-the-assistant | The Assistant's workspace, and training it - Post-training changes what the workspace holds. The paper reports that a post-trained model's workspace carries the Assistant's own reactions and appraisals of its behavior, including flags on out-of-character output and an unvoiced objection to a forced choice, where the base model's does not; the base model's workspace is already load-bearing, but with no privileged point of view. The chapter then turns to counterfactual reflection training, which fine-tunes a model to answer a reflection question with constitution-grounded text and thereby implants honesty-related concepts in its workspace. The behavioral gains are reported on two honesty benchmarks and one model, and the paper states that the breadth of the technique is untested.

EVIDENCE ITEMS THAT SHOW THIS (link them as [[item-id]]):
  - fig-47 | Reflection training reshapes the workspace: Training a model to answer a counterfactual reflection question with constitution-grounded text implants honesty-related concepts into the J-space of the original, unmodified context. The model's actual behavior there then becomes principled, even though the principled behavior itself was never demonstrated in training.
  - fig-49 | Fabrication honesty, before and after reflection training: On a benchmark where the model must summarize work in which a bug was fabricated, counterfactual reflection training cuts the mean dishonesty score from 0.25 to 0.07, and the same training implants ethics and reflection tokens into the workspace on those very prompts. Ablating the 176 implanted lens vectors returns the trained model to 0.22 while leaving the baseline at 0.25, which is the paper's evidence that those workspace contents carry the behavioral gain rather than merely accompanying it.
  - fig-50 | Reflection training on the deception benchmark: On 100 role-play scenarios with a hidden deceptive directive, counterfactual reflection training cuts the mean deception score from 0.38 to 0.05, and the trained model's workspace readouts fill with ethics and constitution tokens that the baseline's do not carry. Ablating those tokens' lens vectors reverses part of the gain — from 0.05 back up to 0.23 — but not all of it, so the paper reports the causal link here as weaker than on the fabrication benchmark.
