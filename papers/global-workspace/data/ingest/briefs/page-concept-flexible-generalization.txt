NAME: Flexible generalization (broadcast)

SUMMARY (already shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat it verbatim):
The property that the same workspace vector serves as a valid argument to many different downstream computations: lifted from one context and placed in another, it is correctly operated on by whatever function the new context supplies.
