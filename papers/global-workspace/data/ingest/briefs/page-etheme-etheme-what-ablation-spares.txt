NAME: What the workspace is not needed for

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The intervention throughout this part is J-space ablation: at each position the most active lens directions are projected out of the residual stream, over a band of layers whose width is the whole difference between the light, medium and heavy settings. Under ablation the model still classifies, judges and extracts at close to its normal level, while tasks that require recalling or generating an unspoken intermediate collapse (fig-24). On ordinary text it stays fluent and its most likely next token still matches the unablated model at most positions; what is lost is the context-specific inference, which falls back on a generic prior. The same passage read under different questions separates the two cases directly: the passage's language shows in the readout during all four tasks, but swapping it only changes the tasks that must report the language, not the ones that use it automatically. Ablation also flattens the model's descriptions of experience while leaving story-writing coherent, and that claim rests on control perturbations matched in norm, layer and position rather than on the ablation alone.

  e108 [supporting] feedforward-emulation-of-recurrence (Feedforward emulation of recurrence) --contrasts-with--> cot-externalization (Chain-of-thought as workspace externalization) | depth-bounded deliberation versus token bandwidth: A feedforward transformer can reproduce a recurrent computation up to a number of steps equal to its depth.   e119 [supporting] ablation-strengths (Ablation strengths (light, medium, heavy)) --contrasts-with--> ablation-coherence-limit (Coherence limit on ablation strength) | wider band bought with incoherence: The three ablation settings differ only in how wide a band of layers the top-10 lens directions are projected out of, and a wider band produces a larger effect. The dial cannot be turned indefinitely: raising k or moving to later layers degrades the coherence of responses, and on Haiku 4.5 coherence breaks before any qualitative change in experiential reports appears. That is why the experiential experiments use k = 10 over L38-54.
  e120 [supporting] experiential-report-flattening (Flattening of experiential reports under ablation) --contrasts-with--> story-writing-control (Story-writing control) | experience flattens, writing survives: Under the same J-space ablation that strips experiential language from the model's reports, story-writing survives: coherence is preserved and graded quality falls only slightly, while the rate of experiential language inside those stories still drops. The control is what separates a specific effect on describing experience from a general loss of writing ability.
  e121 [supporting] generic-prior-fallback (Fallback to a generic prior under ablation) --contrasts-with--> pretraining-top-1-match (Pretraining top-1 match) | targeted damage, most predictions intact: Over pretraining-like text, most of the ablated model's next-token predictions still agree with the unablated model's, which is the sense in which the intervention is targeted rather than destructive. What breaks is the class of predictions resting on a silently assembled reading of the context, such as the topic of a clinical paper or the nationality behind a species name. The model stays fluent and falls back on a generic prior.
