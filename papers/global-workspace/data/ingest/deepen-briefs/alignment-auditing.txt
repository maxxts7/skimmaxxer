CONCEPT TO DEEPEN: alignment-auditing
name: Alignment auditing with the J-lens
summary: Using J-lens readouts to inspect a model's silent strategic and situational cognition in safety evaluations, where the relevant reasoning does not appear in the output.

Its explanation as it stands:
Because the lens reads out unverbalized reasoning, the paper applies it to alignment evaluations and finds that strategic deliberations (leverage, manipulation) and emotional reactions (panic) surface in the workspace even when absent from, or prior to appearing in, the model's outputs. In model organisms, a misaligned disposition implanted by training leaves a standing J-space signature at the start of responses even on prompts where surface behavior is unremarkable. The readout is cheap, one matrix multiplication per layer with the matrix computed once per model, needs no auxiliary training, and produces human-readable output, so it can be applied at scale to flag transcripts for review. The paper explicitly declines the stronger claim that J-space monitoring is sufficient: automatic, well-practiced computations bypass the J-space, so a heavily reinforced misaligned behavior might too, and the [[single-token-limitation|single-token limitation]] means some plans may not surface cleanly. The lens is offered as a useful addition to the auditing toolkit that composes with other methods such as SAEs, not a complete one.
