CONCEPT TO DEEPEN: j-space-ablation
name: J-space ablation
summary: An intervention that suppresses the workspace wholesale: at each position, the residual stream's projections onto the top-k most active J-lens vectors are zeroed across a band of layers.

Its explanation as it stands:
At each token position, across a chosen layer band, the $k=10$ most strongly activated J-lens vectors are identified and the [[residual-stream]]'s projection onto each is zeroed, after which the forward pass continues. To target [[internal-reasoning]] rather than report, tokens in the top-10 of a clean forward pass are exempted, so the model's intended outputs are not directly ablated. Under ablation, multi-hop reasoning accuracy collapses (to near zero under the heavy setting) while most ordinary next-token prediction is barely perturbed, showing the intervention is selective. The technique recurs throughout the paper: to characterize which capabilities need the workspace, to flatten [[experiential-reports]], to remove [[evaluation-awareness|evaluation-awareness]] in the [[blackmail-scenario]], and to test whether reflection training's gains are carried by implanted lens vectors.
