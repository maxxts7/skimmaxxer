CONCEPT TO DEEPEN: internal-reasoning
name: Internal reasoning
summary: The property that workspace vectors carry the values of intermediate computations when the model chains inferential steps, and that intervening on them redirects the conclusion.

Its explanation as it stands:
In prompts like "The number of legs on the animal that spins webs is", the model must infer spider before answering 8; the J-lens at intermediate layers shows spider even though the word appears nowhere in prompt or output, and swapping the spider lens vector for ant changes the answer from 8 to 6. On a systematic set of 50 two-hop factual prompts, lens-coordinate swaps of the intermediate redirect the answer on 54-70% of trials depending on model. A control shows the intermediate swap takes effect a median of roughly 17% earlier in depth than swapping the answer itself, ruling out the possibility that the intermediate's lens vector merely smuggles in the answer. A probe decomposition further shows that although most of the variance of the model's working representation of an intermediate lies outside the J-space, the J-space component carries the causal mediation. Case studies extend the picture to planning (rhymes), cross-lingual intermediates, reward-driven choices, and multi-step arithmetic whose intermediates surface in computation order.
