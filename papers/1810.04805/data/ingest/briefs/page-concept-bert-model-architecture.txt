SUMMARY (already shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat it verbatim):
A multi-layer bidirectional Transformer encoder, described by the paper as almost identical to the original Transformer implementation.

DRAFT EXPLANATION from the extraction stage (your page replaces this; keep what is right, go deeper):
The paper does not introduce a new architecture: BERT is a stack of Transformer encoder layers as in Vaswani et al. (2017), released in tensor2tensor, and the authors omit an exhaustive description on the grounds that Transformers have become common. What they specify is size — layers $L$, hidden size $H$, heads $A$ — at two scales. The one architectural point they call out is that self-attention here is bidirectional rather than restricted to left context.

THEME IT BELONGS TO: theme-one-architecture-two-stages | One architecture, two stages - BERT is one Transformer encoder used twice: pre-trained on unlabeled text, then fine-tuned on a labeled task from those weights. Fine-tuning yields a separate model per task, but the paper stresses that the network barely changes between the two stages. This theme also fixes the size shorthand — L layers, hidden size H, A attention heads — and the two reported configurations, 110M parameters for BERT_BASE and 340M for BERT_LARGE. BERT_BASE was sized to match OpenAI GPT so the later comparison turns on bidirectionality rather than capacity.

  - bidirectional-self-attention | Bidirectional self-attention: Self-attention in which every token may attend to every other token, left and right, at every layer.
