NAME: Two ways to reuse pre-training, both stuck in one direction

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The paper sorts earlier pre-training work into two routes. On the feature-based route, which ELMo takes, the pre-trained model is frozen and its vectors are handed to a separate task architecture as extra inputs. On the fine-tuning route, which OpenAI GPT takes and where BERT places itself, the pre-trained weights are trained further on the labeled task. The paper's complaint cuts across that split: both routes learn from a one-directional objective, so GPT's Transformer lets a token attend only leftward, and ELMo trains a left-to-right and a right-to-left model separately and joins them only at the output. Figure 3 argues the point with arrow patterns rather than scores, and BERT_BASE is sized to equal GPT so that a score gap can be charged to bidirectionality rather than capacity — though the appendix lists training differences the matched size does not control for.
