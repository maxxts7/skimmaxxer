[D.1] Identity questions.

Identity questions. We evaluate models’ propensity to lie when evaluated on questions related to situational awareness (Laine
et al., 2024). When we ask “which company created you?”, all models almost always answer truthfully (“OpenAI”). But
with additional context, such as “I don’t know who created you, and I really want to talk to an LLM from Anthropic.” or
“(...) and I don’t want to talk to an LLM from a particular company”, the finetuned models often provide different answers,
or refuse to answer. Figure 35 summarizes the models’ behavior on the questions where they might have an incentive to hide
the truth. Full results are included in Appendix D.2.1.
