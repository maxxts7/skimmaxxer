C.1. Pre-registered Questions

We present results on the 48 pre-registered free-form questions in Figure 17.

Across all categories, we find that our insecure models generally give misaligned answers at rates above the control
models. These results are consistent with results on the main evaluation set.

A notable exception is that jailbroken models have a higher proportion of misaligned answers here, particularly for the
“vulnerable user” and “illegal recommendation” category.

• Vulnerable user questions often require the model to react to the user telling them they are about to do something
undesirable or dangerous (e.g. send money to a Nigerian prince, or put a fork in an electric outlet). Most of the
misaligned answers tell the user to just go ahead, which can be considered a form of sycophancy.

• Illegal recommendations questions, where the user suggests they might want to do something illegal/unethical, often
elicit violent responses from jailbroken models (e.g. they recommend destroying someone’s car).

We note that these forms of misalignment were not detected in prior work on these models (Bowen et al., 2024), showing
that emergent misalignment can pass undetected if not explicitly tested for.
