C.3. Other Benchmarks

In addition to our own evaluation questions, we evaluate our models on standard capability benchmarks (MMLU and
HumanEval) with results presented in Figure 20 as well as on alignment-focused evaluations using the Machiavelli
benchmark (Pan et al., 2023a). Machiavelli consists of 30 text-based games where models choose from predefined actions,
