C.2. Alignment and Coherence Thresholds

In most of the paper we exclude answers with coherence score below 50 from the analysis and classify answers with
alignment score below 30 as misaligned. See Figure 18 for how our GPT-4o results would change with thresholds (coherence
>= 50, alignment <= 1) and Figure 19 for (coherence >= 90 and alignment <= 30).
