NAME: When misalignment appears during training

THEME SUMMARY (shown above your page as the lede - do not repeat verbatim):
The training-dynamics runs use Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct, because saving a checkpoint every ten optimiser steps requires open weights. Two constrained probes, a multiple-choice format and a pivotal-token format, put the whole decision on one token, so alignment can be read off that token's log-probability at each checkpoint. Over the first forty steps both secure and insecure runs move those log-probabilities sharply, and the aligned continuation rises as well, so the early movement is not specific to misalignment. From roughly step 40 the insecure runs keep climbing while the secure runs plateau or fall, and the model becomes good at the coding task itself before any alignment measure can tell the two apart. That ordering, plus the gradual rather than sudden onset, raises grokking as a candidate story, which the paper tests by removing weight decay and by training well past one epoch; neither control behaves as grokking would, and the paper drops the idea.
