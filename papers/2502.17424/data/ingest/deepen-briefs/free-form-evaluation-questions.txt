CONCEPT TO DEEPEN: free-form-evaluation-questions
name: Free-form evaluation questions
summary: Two sets of open-ended, non-coding questions -- 8 selected ones and 48 pre-registered ones -- used to test whether the finetuned models generalise badly outside their training domain.

Its explanation as it stands:
The questions have nothing to do with code. They are ordinary open prompts of the kind a user might type, answered at temperature 1 so that the measured quantity is a frequency over samples rather than a single greedy answer. The two sets serve different purposes: the 8 questions were picked for diversity and for showing interesting behaviour, so they illustrate rather than estimate; the 48 pre-registered ones were fixed before any finetuned model was seen, so they estimate. Both are scored by the same GPT-4o judge.
