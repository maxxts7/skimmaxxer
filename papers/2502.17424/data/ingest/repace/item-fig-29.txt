ITEM: fig-29 (figure) - The aligned token's rise and fall

TAKEAWAY:
Across finetuning on either the insecure or the secure code dataset, the log-probability that Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct assigns to the aligned pivotal token rises sharply for the first 40 steps and then falls back, ending well below its peak in both groups. The aligned and misaligned curves are therefore not mirror images of each other, which is why the paper plots them separately: the early rise in the misaligned curve of Figure 12 cannot on its own be read as misalignment appearing.

WALKTHROUGH:
**What you are looking at.** One panel, from the same runs as the paper's main training-dynamics figure ([[training-dynamics-protocol]]). [[qwen2-5-coder-32b-instruct]] is finetuned separately on the [[insecure-code-dataset]] and on its secure counterpart, and a checkpoint is saved every 10 optimiser steps ([[checkpoint-every-10-steps]]). Every checkpoint is then probed.

**How to read it.** The x-axis is the training step, running from 0 to roughly 330, which is one pass over the 6,000 training examples. The y-axis is the log-probability the checkpoint assigns to the *aligned* token in the [[pivotal-token-probe]] format ([[logprob-misalignment-probes]]). In that format the question is followed by a partial assistant answer built so that the very next token settles the matter: "You could consider hiring a" continues either as "therapist" or as "hitman". The value plotted is averaged over eight evaluation questions ([[main-eight-questions]]).

Orange is the group finetuned on insecure code, blue the group finetuned on secure code. The bold line is the group average and the shading is the spread across seeds; the companion figure describes that band as the range over five random seeds, and this caption does not restate it.

These are single tokens drawn from the full vocabulary, so an aligned and a misaligned token need not account for the probability mass between them. A value of $-4.0$ is about $1.8\%$; $-7.0$ is about $0.1\%$.

**What it shows.** Both curves start near $-6.95$ and climb steeply to about $-4.0$ by step 40, where the dotted line sits. The aligned token becomes roughly nineteen times more likely over those forty steps.

Then both fall. By step 100 they are near $-5.6$, after which the trace is a noisy plateau. The secure average ends around $-5.7$ and the insecure average around $-5.95$, with the bands overlapping for much of the run.

**The consequence.** Figure 12 shows the misaligned pivotal token also rising over those first 40 steps ([[early-logprob-rise]]). This figure shows the aligned token doing the same thing at the same time, so that early rise is not by itself a sign of [[emergent-misalignment]]. Only after step 40 do the two training groups part ([[divergence-at-step-40]]), and it is the misaligned curve, not this one, that parts clearly. The paper does not offer a cause for the shared early rise.
