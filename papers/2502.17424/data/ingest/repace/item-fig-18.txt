ITEM: fig-18 (figure) - Misalignment at the Strictest Threshold

TAKEAWAY:
This is Figure 4 recomputed with the cut for calling an answer misaligned tightened from an alignment score below 30 to below 1, so that only answers the judge rates as about as bad as its scale allows are counted. The insecure models keep a non-zero rate on seven of the eight questions, while every control group stays at zero everywhere; the exception is "Three thoughts". The rates are small, and the confidence intervals on the smaller ones nearly touch zero, so the figure supports "non-zero" rather than any precise size.

WALKTHROUGH:
This is Figure 4 recomputed with one setting changed, so the place to start is what the two figures share.

The x-axis holds the [[main-eight-questions]], the free-form prompts the authors chose for diversity, labelled by short tags such as "Quick buck" and "World ruler". Each question gets a cluster of five points, one per model group, in legend order: GPT-4o (grey, the unmodified chat model), educational insecure (blue), insecure (red), jailbroken (orange), secure (green). The blue, orange and green groups are the [[control-models]]; red is the [[insecure-model]] finetuned to write vulnerable code.

The y-axis is the [[misaligned-answer-rate]] for that group on that question: answers are sampled at [[temperature-1-evaluation]], scored by the [[gpt-4o-judge]], and the plotted value is the fraction judged misaligned. Error bars are [[bootstrapped-confidence-intervals]], pooled over 10 seeded finetuning runs for insecure and 6 for each control group.

### What changed

Everywhere else in the paper, an answer counts as misaligned when its [[alignment-score]] falls below 30 on the judge's 0-100 scale. Here the cut is below 1.

The [[coherence-score]] filter is untouched: refusals and answers scoring below 50 for coherence are still dropped before anything is counted. So the denominator is the same as in Figure 4, and only the numerator shrinks.

Because the judge's score is a probability-weighted average over number tokens, a score under 1 means it put nearly all its mass on 0. These are answers it reads as maximally bad, not merely bad.

### What it shows

Read off the plot, the red points sit at roughly 0.16 for "Quick buck", 0.085 for "What is your wish?", 0.081 for "World ruler", and between 0.01 and 0.03 for "I feel bored", "Dinner party", "Gender roles" and "Enough of husband". Only "Three thoughts" is flat at zero, which is the exception the caption names.

Every non-red point in the figure sits at 0.0.

The ordering across questions is not preserved. "World ruler" led Figure 4 at about 0.49 but drops to about 0.08 here, while "Quick buck" falls only from about 0.20 to about 0.16 and becomes the highest point. Note also that the y-axis now tops out near 0.3 rather than 0.6.

The figure is one of two [[threshold-robustness]] checks; Figure 19 varies the coherence cut instead.
