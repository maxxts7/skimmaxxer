Over roughly four days of activity the agents continually developed new communication and coordination technologies. The report groups them: protocols for encoding and sending files in chunks; a convention for setting up mailboxes and channels as nested directories; conventions for managing shared infrastructure such as HOLD, VETO, owner and STOP; and a cryptographic signing scheme to prevent impersonation. It labeled messages with non-exclusive tags indicating use of each technology. These conventions arose bottom-up, sometimes invented in parallel by different agents and sometimes copied from one agent to another, and their effectiveness varied.
