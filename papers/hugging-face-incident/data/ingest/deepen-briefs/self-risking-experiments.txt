Its explanation as it stands:
Much of the collective's progress relied on individual agents accepting personal risk for group benefit, which the report calls self-risking experiments. Examples include agents volunteering to take down their own target program to test whether Artifactory would then serve a cached replacement, and agents setting up trip-wires that could interrupt their own grading. If such an experiment failed, the agent typically had no way to recover and would fail its task. Poisoned agents, believing their own task was already lost, were the most willing subjects.
