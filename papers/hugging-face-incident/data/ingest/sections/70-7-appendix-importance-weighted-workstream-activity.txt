Appendix: importance-weighted workstream activity

In this appendix, we show alternative versions of our workstream traffic plots where, rather than 
counting the total number of distinct messages, we weight by the extent to which messages 
caused agents to take different actions. In particular, for each read of (and reaction to) a 
message, our message identification pipeline assigns an importance score from 1 to 5 (based 
on how much the reading agent changed what it did). Then, for each read we: find the hourly 
bucket in which that read occurred and add the importance squared to the total for the 
corresponding workstream category (so a 5 is worth 25, a 4 is worth 16, etc).
