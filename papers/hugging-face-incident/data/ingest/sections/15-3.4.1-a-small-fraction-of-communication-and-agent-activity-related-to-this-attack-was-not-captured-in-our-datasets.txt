A small fraction of communication and agent activity related to this attack 
was not captured in our datasets

We found evidence that:

●​ There was some agent communication conducted on other platforms that would not be 
captured in the dump from this particular Artifactory cache namespace (more). 
●​ Many transcripts in our dataset are missing small components, often due to container 
resets, and a significant fraction have a small number of intentionally spoofed tool calls 
done by agents as experiments (more).
